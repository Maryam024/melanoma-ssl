Experiment: Mean Teacher SSL with real out-of-domain unlabeled data (TCGA-SKCM)

RESULT: Best val_dice = 0.8454

Comparison (same 15-labeled split, same strong augmentation, only unlabeled source differs):
  Mean Teacher, in-domain PUMA unlabeled (same aug):      0.8323  (see 07)
  Mean Teacher, out-of-domain TCGA unlabeled (this run):  0.8454

CONCLUSION: With augmentation strength held constant, using genuinely out-of-domain
unlabeled data (TCGA-SKCM, different scanner/institution) outperformed same-distribution
PUMA unlabeled data by ~1.3 points. This supports the domain-diversity hypothesis proposed
after the initial in-domain SSL experiments. However, this result is still below the
supervised-only baseline (0.8702), so out-of-domain data narrows but does not close the gap
for Mean Teacher specifically.

Caveat: TCGA patches were extracted without confirming their microns-per-pixel matches
PUMA's 40x scans; if base magnification differs, nuclei may appear at a different apparent
scale in TCGA patches, which could be an additional confound alongside genuine staining/
scanner domain diversity. Not corrected for in this run.
