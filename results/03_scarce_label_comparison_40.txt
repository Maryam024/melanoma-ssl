Experiment: Semi-supervised learning comparison at moderate label scarcity
Data: 40 labeled / 140 unlabeled / 25 held-out val (all from PUMA, same distribution)
Config: 60 epochs, batch_size=2, mixed precision, lr=5e-5

--- Supervised-only, random init, 40 labeled examples ---
Best val_dice = 0.8903

--- Mean Teacher SSL (consistency regularization), 40 labeled + 140 unlabeled ---
Best val_dice = 0.8883

CONCLUSION: Consistent with the 15-labeled experiment — SSL did not improve
over supervised-only at this labeled-set size either. Two independent trials
(15 and 40 labeled) both show the same small negative gap, suggesting this
is a real pattern rather than single-run noise.
